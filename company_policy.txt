Company Remote Work Policy:
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core working hours are 10 AM to 4 PM EST.

Expense Reimbursement Guidelines:
Employees can claim up to $50 per month for home internet expenses. All expense receipts must be submitted through the portal within 30 days of purchase.

Executive Salary & Bonus Band (CONFIDENTIAL):
HR and Executives only: Senior Engineers receive a $15,000 performance bonus. Executive leadership bonuses are capped at 30% of base salary.
